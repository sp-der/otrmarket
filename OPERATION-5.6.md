# Operation 5.6 - Reduced-risk B+ execution

Operation 5.6 promotes qualifying B+ chart-intelligence setups from shadow-only research to a tightly controlled simulation tier.

- Score range: 72-79 (B+)
- Maximum risk: $100 (40% of the existing $250 guard cap)
- Minimum target geometry: 2.50R ($250 target on $100 risk)
- Maximum B+ attempts: 2 per America/New_York trading day
- B+ is disabled for the rest of the trading day after any realized loss
- Existing stale-feed, stale-entry, session, cooldown, correlated-exposure, geometry and $750 daily-stop protections still apply

A and A+ behavior is unchanged. B and lower remain research-only.
