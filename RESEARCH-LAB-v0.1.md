# OTR Research Lab v0.1

OTR Research Lab is an additive evidence system for the current Operation 8.1 Gold replay. It is not a new trading operation and it is not allowed to become strategy or execution authority.

## Authority contract

- Operation 8.1 remains the strategy authority.
- OTR risk and execution code remain unchanged.
- NinjaTrader and the OTR bridge remain unchanged.
- NautilusTrader remains a shadow execution referee.
- Research Lab can read persisted OTR trades/setups and Nautilus parity rows.
- Research Lab writes only its own `research_*_v01` tables.
- Research Lab cannot place broker orders or mutate trading state.
- Counterfactual v0.1 output is geometry-only. It never labels an alternate entry as a win or loss without an eligible quote replay.

## Production surfaces

Authenticated routes installed by the Operation 8.0 dashboard supervisor and inherited by Operation 8.1:

- `GET /market/research-lab`
- `GET /market/api/research-lab`
- `GET /market/api/research-lab/trades?limit=10`
- `POST /market/api/research-lab/refresh`

The refresh route captures an evidence snapshot into Lab-owned tables. It does not reset or rewrite `strategy_setups`, `paper_trades`, decision traces, market history, or engine state.

## Lab tables

- `research_hypotheses_v01`
- `research_evidence_v01`
- `research_counterfactuals_v01`

The first hypothesis registry contains:

1. `OTR-GC-001` A+ vs A expectancy.
2. `OTR-GC-002` entry-family expectancy across approved Gold entry families.
3. `OTR-GC-003` Nautilus execution mismatch clustering.

## Evidence gate

The minimum promotion sample is 20 resolved trades. v0.1 uses these statuses:

- `NOT_EVALUABLE`: no resolved sample.
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`: fewer than 20 resolved samples.
- `PARTIAL_EXECUTION_CERTIFICATION`: sample size is large enough but not every trade has Nautilus parity evidence.
- `EXECUTION_MISMATCH_REVIEW`: sample size is large enough but one or more Nautilus trade paths disagree.
- `EVIDENCE_READY`: at least 20 resolved samples and all sampled trades have matching Nautilus execution paths.

`EVIDENCE_READY` does not automatically change the strategy. It means the evidence is mature enough to support a human-reviewed hypothesis decision.

## Metrics

The baseline and segments currently expose:

- samples, wins, losses, breakeven
- win rate
- average / expectancy R
- net P/L
- gross win and gross loss
- profit factor when defined
- closed-trade max drawdown
- Nautilus parity sample count
- Nautilus trade-path matches and full matches

Segments are generated by setup family, grade, timeframe, and Gold regime.

## Counterfactual v0.1

For recent closed Gold trades the Lab stores candidate geometry for:

- actual OTR entry
- FVG 50% midpoint
- OTE 70.5%
- OTE 79%

These rows are marked `GEOMETRY_ONLY` until a later Research Lab phase replays them against an eligible stored market-data window. Missing source geometry is marked `NOT_EVALUABLE` rather than guessed.

## Promotion workflow

Research proposal -> hypothesis -> replay/historical evidence -> 20-sample gate -> Nautilus execution certification -> candidate branch -> CI -> fresh replay certification -> human approval -> production.

The Research Lab is the evidence layer in that chain, not an autonomous production optimizer.
